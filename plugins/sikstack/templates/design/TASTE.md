# TASTE: <product>

The product's one-page taste file, `<design folder>/TASTE.md`. The drafter reads it before every draft, and the
critic judges against it. Keep it to one page: what the tokens and components cannot say. Every line has a source.

## The operator's standing rules

Quoted verbatim, each with its date and where it was said. Only the operator's own words go here; a rule they
replace is removed, with the new one saying what it replaced.

1. **<the rule, in one line>**
   - "<their words>" The operator, <YYYY-MM-DD>, <where: a phase, a stop, a review>.

## The look

A few lines, each citing its source (a signed design, the tokens file, a decision record):

- **Mood:** <one line: what the product should feel like, and what it must not>.
- **Type:** <the faces and the steps, from the tokens file>.
- **Space and shape:** <the control height, the radii, the column>.
- **Colour:** <what the accent is for; what never carries meaning alone>.
- **Copy:** <the voice: case, length, words the product uses and words it never uses>.
